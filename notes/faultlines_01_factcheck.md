# Fault Lines #1: fact-check (appended 19 Oct 2026, 19:30 Copenhagen time)
Main sources: Wikipedia "Assassination of Charlie Kirk" (revision of 15 Oct 2026, 22:54 UTC), cross-checked against the primary news sources it cites (NBC News 1 Sep 2026, NPR 16 Sep 2025, AP, Reuters, NYT, Salt Lake Tribune, Deseret News, Guardian). **[U]** = uncertain or unverified; do not state these as fact on air.

## A. The Kirk assassination
**Established (multiple reliable sources):**
- **When/where:** 10 Sep 2025, about 12:23 MDT, Utah Valley University, Orem, Utah, during an outdoor Turning Point USA campus debate with about 3,000 people present. Kirk (31) was hit in the neck by a single rifle shot from the roof of the Losee Center, about 142 yards (130 m) away, and was pronounced dead at hospital.
- **Last exchange:** student Hunter Kozak asked how many transgender Americans had been mass shooters in the last 10 years ("Too many," Kirk said). Kozak said "It's five" and asked how many mass shooters there had been in total. Kirk replied "Counting or not counting gang violence?", and the shot followed. (Wikipedia, citing NPR/Deseret News.)
- **Suspect:** Tyler James Robinson, 22, of Washington, Utah, an electrical apprentice from a Republican LDS family. He surrendered on the evening of 11 Sep 2025 after his parents recognised him from FBI images, with a family friend (a retired deputy) arranging it. The manhunt lasted 33 hours. He had no criminal record, was registered to vote but unaffiliated, and has no voting record.
- **Charges:** filed 16 Sep 2025. Aggravated murder; felony discharge of a firearm; 2 counts of obstruction of justice; 2 counts of witness tampering; 3 victim-targeting enhancements; and violence committed in the presence of a child (10 counts in total). **The state is seeking the death penalty**, alleging he targeted Kirk for his political expression (NPR 16 Sep 2025; Deseret News 24 Sep 2025).
- **Legal status now:** preliminary hearing held 6-10 Jul 2026. On **1 Sep 2026** Judge Tony Graf ruled that he will stand trial for aggravated murder, and **Robinson pleaded not guilty to all counts** (NBC News, 1 Sep 2026). His next appearance was set for 23 Oct 2026, this Friday. **No trial date is known.** Robinson is **presumed innocent; refer to him as the accused/alleged shooter.** Other events: in Mar 2026 the defence cited an ATF analysis that could not conclusively tie a bullet fragment to the rifle (Politifact, 2 Apr 2026); in May 2026 the judge allowed cameras in court; on 26 Jun 2026 a prosecutor was held in contempt over a publicity order, and the defence's bid to drop the death penalty was denied (CNN). Erika Kirk filed a speedy-trial motion (Jan 2026).
- **What prosecutors allege about motive (allegations, untested at trial):** a text to his roommate/partner, "I had enough of his hatred. Some hate can't be negotiated out."; telling his father Kirk "spreads too much hate"; his mother told investigators he had moved "more to the left, becoming more pro-gay and trans rights–oriented" (prosecutor Jeffrey Gray, 16 Sep 2025). Governor Spencer Cox (R) said Robinson held "leftist ideology", without giving specifics.
- **Bullet casings** (FBI briefing, 12 Sep 2025): a mix of memes and pop culture, including "hey fascist! CATCH! ↑→↓↓↓" (a Helldivers 2 reference), "O Bella ciao, Bella ciao..." (the anti-fascist song) and furry/troll jokes. Ironic internet culture, not a clear manifesto.
- **Pre-assassination quote** (widely circulated afterwards, sourced): Kirk, April 2023 TPUSA event in Salt Lake City: "I think it's worth it, I think it's worth to have a cost of, unfortunately, some gun deaths every single year so that we can have the Second Amendment to protect our other God-given rights." (Wikipedia "Charlie Kirk", citing contemporary reports.) Use only verbatim, in context.

**Uncertain / contested [U]:**
- **[U] The precise motive.** It is not established in court. The prosecution narrative is "hatred" of Kirk's views, plus the mother's account of a leftward, pro-trans-rights shift. Trump and other Republicans claimed links to organised left-wing groups; **as of 21 Sep 2025 investigators had reportedly found no such evidence**, and I found no later confirmation. Do not say "a left-wing network" or "a lone leftist" as fact; the guests may *claim* either.
- **[U] Whether his statements count as a confession** is for the court to decide (prosecutor Gray).
- **[U] Conspiracy theories** (Candace Owens; a "Groyper"/far-right motive; Israeli involvement; "transgender ideology" claims): Wikipedia treats them as unsupported or misinformation. Present them only as claims, and debunk them.
- **[U] Poll numbers** vary a lot by wording. For example YouGov (12 Sep 2025): 87% say political violence is a problem, and "can sometimes be justified" was 18% of liberals vs 7% of conservatives. Bright Line Watch: under 5% condone violent felonies, with little party difference. Always name the poll and its wording.

**Reactions (established, all sourced):**
- **Bipartisan condemnation:** Trump, Vance, Bush, Johnson, Thune; Obama, Biden, Clinton, Schumer, Jeffries, Newsom, Omar. Memorial on 21 Sep 2025 at State Farm Stadium, Arizona, where **Erika Kirk publicly forgave the killer**. Posthumous Presidential Medal of Freedom.
- **The right:** Trump's address blamed only "the radical left" and did not mention Democratic victims (NBC called it "far more polarizing" than other messages). On 15 Sep 2025 the administration threatened a crackdown on liberal groups and donors (talk of "terrorist" labels; Vance named the Open Society and Ford foundations). About 100 philanthropies signed an open letter in protest. Vance, hosting Kirk's show, urged people to report Kirk-celebrators to their employers. Laura Loomer and others doxxed alleged celebrants. The "Expose Charlie's Murderers" site (later the "Charlie Kirk Data Foundation") collected over 63,000 submissions and was taken offline on 16 Sep 2025. Some people were **misidentified** (e.g. a Wisconsin teacher, a Walmart IT worker).
- **Firings (notable controversy):** widespread firings and suspensions of teachers, firefighters, military and Secret Service staff, academics and others over posts celebrating the death, or merely criticising Kirk. The NYT described it as a conservative "cancel culture". Some cases later ended in settlements (e.g. a Tennessee professor, NYT 1 Jul 2026 **[U: details not read]**).
- **Kimmel:** after his 15 Sep 2025 monologue ("MAGA gang" trying to paint the killer "as anything other than one of them"), FCC chair Brendan Carr threatened ABC. Nexstar pre-empted the show and ABC suspended it (17 Sep 2025); it returned days later after a backlash. A free-speech flashpoint for both guests.
- **The left:** condemnation from leaders, alongside online celebration or "no sympathy" posts from some users. Democrats and analysts pointed to Trump's rhetoric, to violence against Democrats (the Hortman killing, Jun 2025) and to research showing most US ideologically motivated killings since 1990 were by right-wing extremists. The DOJ then removed a 2024 NIJ study saying so (reported; **[U] confirm the removal date before quoting**).
- **Europe:** right-wing populists, including Orbán, used the killing to denounce the left. **[U] I found no Danish reactions; check before mentioning Denmark in this context.**

## B. Boxell, Gentzkow & Shapiro: the Denmark claim (re-verified)
**Verdict: CONFIRMED, with caveats.** NBER WP 26669 (Jan 2020, rev. Nov 2021; published in *REStat* 2024), read from the PDF:
- p.3: "the US exhibited the largest increase in affective polarization over this period. In five other countries—Switzerland, France, Denmark, Canada, and New Zealand—polarization also rose, but to a lesser extent. In six other countries—Japan, Australia, Britain, Norway, Sweden, and (West) Germany—polarization fell."
- p.8: "The trend is statistically significant for Denmark." The Danish slope is about 0.21 points per year on the original scale (Fig. 1 panel), vs the US at 5.6 points per decade.
- **Caveats:** the Danish data are Danish Election Study "sympathy/like" thermometers from 1971-2015 (appendix A.5.5), so the study says **nothing about 2016-2026** (Paludan, the Quran law, the SVM centre government). Its measure is feelings toward parties, not toward voters. Say: "Denmark's polarization rose, much less than America's, up to 2015." Norway and Sweden fell over the same period, which shows the Nordics are not one case.
