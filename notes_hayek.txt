Friedrich Hayek, The Road to Serfdom (1944). Main thesis: central economic planning, even well-intentioned, concentrates power, requires coercion over individual choices, and tends toward totalitarianism; the rule of law and markets preserve freedom.
Counter-argument (give it a strong voice): Keynes wrote Hayek a letter agreeing morally but saying the question is where to draw the line, and that moderate planning in a society that shares right values is safe. The Nordic countries combined high taxes, big welfare states and active labour market policy with high freedom, democracy and rule of law, with no slide into serfdom.
Wrap-up: what Hayek got right (knowledge problem, power concentration), what the Nordic experience suggests, and a takeaway for listeners.
