# Fault Lines #1: Carter's question set (anchored on the Kirk assassination)
Episode: "Left vs right in the contemporary West: is it becoming a war? Why the rise in antagonism?" Object of focus: the assassination of Charlie Kirk, Utah Valley University, 10 Sep 2025.
Cast: Host Carter | Guest = Elliot (far right) | Guest2 = Clara (far left). cast.json seed 548870699; cast_traits.json seed 275136507.
Rules (content_style v2.4): use about 6-9 of these in any order; at least 2-3 must **evolve live** from an outburst, slip or bias. Afterwards, mark each one used / dropped / evolved. Facts: see ep1_factcheck.md. Robinson is the *accused*; he pleaded not guilty and has not been tried.

1. **(Cold-open bait)** "A man is shot mid-sentence answering a question about gun violence, at a campus *debate*. Is that the moment it became a war, or are we making one killing into a war because it suits us?"
2. **(Reactions as a mirror)** "Forget the shooter for a second. What did your *own* side's reaction to the killing reveal about your side: the celebration posts, or the firings and the lists?"
3. **(Schmitt)** "Schmitt says the enemy is public, not personal, and that the concept means the 'real possibility of physical killing'. Was Kirk an enemy in that sense to somebody? Is he one to you?"
4. **(Moralisation, Mouffe)** "Mouffe says we have swapped 'right versus left' for 'right versus wrong'. 'Some hate can't be negotiated out' is what the prosecutors say the accused texted. Is that sentence the logic of your side too?"
5. **(Adversary or enemy)** "Could you call the other side's people *adversaries*, legitimate opponents, and mean it? What would you have to give up to say that?"
6. **(The data, Kalmoe & Mason)** "In their 2021 survey, about one in ten partisans on *both* sides said killing opposing political leaders was at least 'a little bit' justified. Clara, Elliot: are you in that ten percent? Be honest."
7. **(Who's more violent?)** "The right points to Kirk; the left points to Melissa Hortman and January 6, and to the research saying most US political killings since 1990 came from the right. Is counting bodies an argument, or just another weapon?"
8. **(Free speech after the killing)** "Teachers fired, Kimmel pulled off air, a website collecting 63,000 posts. Accountability, or the right's own cancel culture? And would the left have done exactly the same?"
9. **(Why now, Mason)** "We don't disagree that much more on policy than we used to. We just hate each other more. So what changed: sorting, phones, money, immigration, the end of the factory and the church?"
10. **(Europe and Denmark)** "Is this an American disease, or are we next? Denmark's polarization rose, but only a little, and Norway's and Sweden's fell. Is Danish consensus a vaccine, or, as Mouffe would say, the thing that breeds the backlash?"
11. **(Erika Kirk's forgiveness)** "His widow forgave the killer in public. Is forgiveness politically possible for your side, or only privately?"
12. **(Personal)** "Who in your own life do you no longer talk to because of politics, and do you miss them?"
